## 🧠 **The GBrain Architecture That Scales**

### **Multi-Domain Management Pattern:**
```
Y Combinator Scale Brain Architecture:
├── people/ → 4,383 individuals across entire portfolio
├── companies/ → 723 companies with full operational context
├── deals/ → Investment pipeline across multiple verticals
├── meetings/ → Automated ingestion with cross-company attendee enrichment  
├── ops/ → Task and priority management across all domains
├── ideas/ → Cross-portfolio pattern recognition and trend analysis
└── briefing/ → Daily intelligence compilation across entire ecosystem
```

### **What Makes This Scale Possible:**

#### **1. MECE Directory Structure (Prevents Chaos at Scale)**
- **Every entity has exactly one primary home** (no duplicate pages across 723 companies)
- **Resolver decision tree** for filing intelligence across multiple domains
- **Explicit disambiguation rules** when companies/people span multiple categories
- **Inbox/ overflow** for items that don't fit current schema (signals evolution needed)

#### **2. Compiled Truth + Timeline (Institutional Memory That Compounds)**
- **Above the line:** Current state across all 723 companies (always rewritten)
- **Below the line:** Append-only historical timeline (never edit, preserves context)  
- **Cross-references:** Automatic linking across entire portfolio ecosystem
- **Source attribution:** Every fact traceable to original communication

#### **3. Iron Law Back-Linking (Relationship Integrity at Scale)**
- **Every mention creates connections** across all 4,383 people and 723 companies
- **Bidirectional links maintained** automatically across entire portfolio
- **Broken links = broken brain** - mandatory relationship preservation
- **Cross-company relationship intelligence** preserved through personnel movement
